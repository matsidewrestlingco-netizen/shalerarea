A Note from the Board

This season didn't just move the program forward; it raised the standard.

We took on more than ever before: three major tournaments, the addition of our Girls Varsity team to the board, coaching transitions, and continued growth across every level of the program. None of that happens without the people behind the scenes showing up, stepping up, and getting it done.

To our coaches: Thank you for your leadership, your consistency, and the example you set every single day. You're not just building wrestlers; you're building tough, disciplined, and confident young people. That impact goes far beyond the mat.

To our parents: This year demanded a lot, and you delivered. From long tournament days to organizing, volunteering, and supporting in every way imaginable, you helped make all three events a success. You kept this program running at a high level. That kind of commitment is what separates good programs from great ones.

What we accomplished this year matters, but how we did it matters even more. This is a community that shows up for each other, and it shows in everything we do.

We're proud of where we are, and even more excited about where we're headed.

Thank you for being the backbone of Shaler Area Wrestling.
