Tournament Format

We are excited to welcome 12 teams from across western Pennsylvania to the 2026 Titan Duals. Brackets, live scoring, and team standings will be on FloArena.

  • Teams are split into two pools of six. Each team wrestles every team in its pool (5 duals), then one placement dual, for a total of 6 duals per team.
  • All duals will be wrestled on Mats 1 through 4.
  • Pool standings and tiebreakers are determined by FloArena.

Placement Round

After pool play, each team wrestles the team that finished in the same place in the other pool.
